# Week 9 Grammar — City, Transport & Directions

## 1. City Vocabulary & Accusative with 'es gibt'

### Concept & Purpose
Use 'Gibt es hier...' + Accusative to ask about places in town (einen Bahnhof, eine Apotheke, ein Krankenhaus).

### Example Sentences
| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Gibt es hier einen Supermarkt? — Ja, in der Hauptstraße. | Example sentence illustrating the rule. |

### Golden Rules & Checklist
- Always verify the verb position (finite verb on Position 2 in declarative main clauses).
- In modal verb sentences and Perfekt, keep the second verb (Infinitive / Partizip II) at the very end.
- Watch case endings: Nominative (Subject), Accusative (Direct Object), Dative (Location / Indirect Object).

## 3. Location Prepositions & Contractions with Dative

### Concept & Purpose
in + dem = im, an + dem = am, bei + dem = beim, von + dem = vom, zu + dem = zum, zu + der = zur.

### Example Sentences
| German | English |
| --- | --- |
