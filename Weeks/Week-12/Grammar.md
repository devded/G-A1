# Week 12 Grammar — Goethe A1 Exam Prep & Mock Exams

## 1. Goethe A1 Exam Structure & Scoring Matrix

### Concept & Purpose
The exam consists of 4 modules: Hören (20 min, 15 pts), Lesen (25 min, 15 pts), Schreiben (20 min, 15 pts), Sprechen (15 min, 15 pts). Total: 60 pts scaled to 100%. Pass threshold is 60%.

### Example Sentences
| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Ergebnis: 60% = Bestanden, 70% = Befriedigend, 80% = Gut, 90%+ = Sehr gut. | Example sentence illustrating the rule. |

### Golden Rules & Checklist
- Always verify the verb position (finite verb on Position 2 in declarative main clauses).
- In modal verb sentences and Perfekt, keep the second verb (Infinitive / Partizip II) at the very end.
- Watch case endings: Nominative (Subject), Accusative (Direct Object), Dative (Location / Indirect Object).

## 3. Case System Master Summary (Nominative, Accusative, Dative)

### Concept & Purpose
