# Week 12 Mixed Exercises

## Vocabulary

1. Write the German for **the exam / examination** (include article for nouns).
2. Write the German for **the certificate** (include article for nouns).
3. Write the German for **the part / section** (include article for nouns).
4. Write the German for **the task / exercise** (include article for nouns).
5. Write the German for **the question** (include article for nouns).
6. Write the German for **the answer** (include article for nouns).
7. Write the German for **correct / right / true** (include article for nouns).
8. Write the German for **wrong / false** (include article for nouns).

## Grammar

1. Write a correct statement using the week's first grammar topic.
2. Change it into a question where possible.
3. Write a negative version using *nicht* or *kein*.
4. Write one sentence beginning with a time or place expression (Position 1).
5. Correct the word order: `Morgen ich fahre nach Berlin.`

## Translation

Translate into German using only familiar A1 structures:

1. Where is the nearest train station / pharmacy?
2. I would like to buy a ticket, please.
3. Go straight ahead and turn right at the traffic lights.
4. I have to work today, but tomorrow I can visit you.
5. Write one personal sentence connected to Week 12.

## Integrated Output

Complete one 60-second speaking response and one 50-word writing response for this milestone:

> Master exam strategies for Listening (Hören), Reading (Lesen), Writing (Schreiben), and Speaking (Sprechen), and complete two full A1 Mock Exams.
