# Week 10 Speaking

## Weekly outcomes

- master key situational patterns for Weather, Seasons & Modal Verbs
- ask and answer questions confidently
- handle polite requests and information exchanges
- self-correction and communication repair

## Daily five-minute drill

1. Answer three familiar questions connected to Week 10.
2. Ask the same questions using both informal `du` and formal `Sie`.
3. Add one detail: time, place, quantity, or reason.
4. Repair one simulated misunderstanding (*Wie bitte? Können Sie das wiederholen?*).

## Role-plays

1. **Information exchange:** Introduce the weekly topic and ask three questions.
2. **Practical task:** One person requests information or service; the other responds.
3. **Problem resolution:** One detail is unavailable, wrong, or delayed; negotiate a simple alternative.

## Recording checkpoint

Speak for 90–120 seconds on the weekly milestone: **Describe weather conditions, talk about seasons and free-time plans, and use modal verbs with correct sentence brackets.**. Use at least eight active words and three sentence patterns. Record twice, correcting your main errors between attempts.

## Self-rating rubric

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task completion | not completed | partly completed | all required points addressed |
| Understandability | mostly unclear | understandable with effort | clear pronunciation and flow |
| Vocabulary | isolated words | some phrases | appropriate range for topic |
| Grammar | meaning blocked | errors but meaning clear | controlled A1 sentence patterns |
| Interaction | no response | limited | asks and responds appropriately |
