# Week 10 Grammar — Weather, Seasons & Modal Verbs

## 1. Impersonal 'es' for Weather Expressions

### Concept & Purpose
German uses impersonal 'es' for weather: 'Es regnet', 'Es schneit', 'Es ist sonnig/kalt', 'Es sind 20 Grad'.

### Example Sentences
| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Wie ist das Wetter heute? — Es ist sonnig und warm. | Example sentence illustrating the rule. |

### Golden Rules & Checklist
- Always verify the verb position (finite verb on Position 2 in declarative main clauses).
- In modal verb sentences and Perfekt, keep the second verb (Infinitive / Partizip II) at the very end.
- Watch case endings: Nominative (Subject), Accusative (Direct Object), Dative (Location / Indirect Object).

## 2. Preposition 'im' with Seasons and Months

### Concept & Purpose
Use 'im' (in + dem) for seasons (im Sommer, im Winter) and months (im Januar, im Juli).

## 3. Modal Verbs: können (can) & müssen (must)

### Concept & Purpose
Modal verbs conjugate irregularly in singular (ich kann, du kannst, er kann / ich muss, du musst, er muss).

### Example Sentences
| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Ich kann heute nicht kommen, denn ich muss arbeiten. | Example sentence illustrating the rule. |

## 4. Modal Verbs: wollen (want to) & dürfen (be allowed to)

### Concept & Purpose

## 5. Modal Verb Sentence Bracket (Satzklammer)

### Concept & Purpose
The conjugated modal verb takes Position 2; the main verb in infinitive goes to the very end of the clause.

### Example Sentences
| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Subjekt (1) + Modalverb (2) + Angaben (Mittelfeld) + Infinitiv (Ende): 'Ich möchte heute Abend Pizza essen.' | Example sentence illustrating the rule. |
