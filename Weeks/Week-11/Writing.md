# Week 11 Writing

## Weekly outcomes

- fill out situational forms and tables
- write short emails, notes, and messages (30–60 words)
- verify required content points and punctuation

## Progression tasks

1. Write five isolated words from memory with articles and plurals where applicable.
2. Write five correct simple sentences on the theme of **Health & Present Perfect Tense**.
3. Connect two sentences with connectors (*und, aber, oder, denn, deshalb*).
4. Write one practical message or email connected to the weekly milestone.
5. Correct and rewrite the text using the checklist below.

## Weekly prompt

Write 40–80 words demonstrating: **Describe health symptoms, make doctor appointments, understand pharmacy instructions, and express past events using the Perfekt tense.**

Include:
- all required personal or practical information
- at least six weekly vocabulary items
- at least two target grammar structures
- one question, request, or proposal
- appropriate greeting and closing formula

## Correction checklist

- [ ] Every sentence contains a conjugated finite verb.
- [ ] The finite verb is in Position 2 (statements) or Position 1 (yes/no questions/imperatives).
- [ ] All nouns and formal pronouns (*Sie, Ihnen, Ihr*) begin with capital letters.
- [ ] Articles match gender and grammatical case.
- [ ] All required content points are addressed.
- [ ] The final version is cleanly rewritten.
