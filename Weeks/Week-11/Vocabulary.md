# Week 11 Vocabulary — Health & Present Perfect Tense

**Target:** 48 lexical items. Active items should be produced in speech and writing; recognition items should be understood in context.

## Learning rule

- Nouns: article + singular + plural (e.g. *der Bahnhof, die Bahnhöfe*)
- Verbs: infinitive + conjugated example sentence
- Phrases: learn as fixed communicative chunks
- Review schedule: same day, +1, +3, +7, +14, and +30 days
