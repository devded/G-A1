# Week 11 Pronunciation

## Goals

- clear pace and rhythm
- sentence stress and question intonation (rising vs falling)
- accurate consonant clusters and vowel lengths
- independent self-correction

## Daily routine

1. Select five assigned words containing the target feature.
2. Listen to the local audio and repeat slowly twice.
3. Repeat at natural native speed twice.
4. Put each word in a short communicative sentence.
5. Record and compare one pronunciation feature only.

## Bengali-speaker attention points

- Keep German long and short vowels distinct (e.g. *bieten* vs *bitten*, *fühlen* vs *füllen*).
- Do not insert intrusive vowels inside consonant clusters (e.g. *Straße*, *Sport*, *sprechen*).
- Treat German `w` as voiced labiodental /v/ (not English /w/).
- Treat `z` consistently as /ts/ (e.g. *Zug*, *Zahn*, *Zeit*).
- Practice rounded umlauts `ö` and `ü` with proper lip rounding.
