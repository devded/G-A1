# Week 11 Grammar — Health & Present Perfect Tense

## 1. Expressing Pain & Symptoms (tut weh / Schmerzen)

### Concept & Purpose
Singular: 'Mein Kopf tut weh.' Plural: 'Meine Beine tun weh.' Compound pain nouns: Kopfschmerzen, Bauchschmerzen, Zahnschmerzen haben.

### Example Sentences
| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Ich habe Halsschmerzen und mein Rücken tut weh. | Example sentence illustrating the rule. |

### Golden Rules & Checklist
- Always verify the verb position (finite verb on Position 2 in declarative main clauses).
- In modal verb sentences and Perfekt, keep the second verb (Infinitive / Partizip II) at the very end.
- Watch case endings: Nominative (Subject), Accusative (Direct Object), Dative (Location / Indirect Object).

## 3. Present Perfect (Perfekt) with 'haben' (Regular Verbs)

### Concept & Purpose
Form: haben (Pos 2) + ge-[Verbstamm]-t (Ende). e.g., lernen -> gelernt, kaufen -> gekauft, arbeiten -> gearbeitet.

### Example Sentences
| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Ich habe gestern zwei Stunden Deutsch gelernt. | Example sentence illustrating the rule. |

### Golden Rules & Checklist

- In modal verb sentences and Perfekt, keep the second verb (Infinitive / Partizip II) at the very end.
- Watch case endings: Nominative (Subject), Accusative (Direct Object), Dative (Location / Indirect Object).

## 4. Present Perfect (Perfekt) with Irregular Verbs

### Concept & Purpose
Irregular verbs form Partizip II with ge-...-en and often change stem vowels: essen -> gegessen, trinken -> getrunken, schreiben -> geschrieben.

### Example Sentences

## 5. Present Perfect (Perfekt) with 'sein' (Movement & State Change)

### Concept & Purpose
Verbs of movement/change of state use 'sein' as auxiliary: fahren -> ist gefahren, gehen -> ist gegangen, kommen -> ist gekommen, aufstehen -> ist aufgestanden, bleiben -> ist geblieben.

### Example Sentences
| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Ich bin um 7 Uhr aufgestanden und nach Berlin gefahren. | Example sentence illustrating the rule. |

### Golden Rules & Checklist

- In modal verb sentences and Perfekt, keep the second verb (Infinitive / Partizip II) at the very end.
- Watch case endings: Nominative (Subject), Accusative (Direct Object), Dative (Location / Indirect Object).
