# Writing Roadmap

| Week | Theme & Writing Product | Length |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Personal registration form and basic introduction profile | 4–6 sentences |
| 2 | Personal background, origin, languages and contact info | 6–8 sentences |
| 3 | Family description and possessive relationships | 6–8 sentences |
| 4 | Apartment layout, room description and furniture inventory | 8–10 sentences |
| 5 | Job profile, workplace description and business contact | 8–10 sentences |
| 6 | Daily routine schedule, meeting arrangement and invitation | short message / email |
| 7 | Hobbies, weekend activity plans and diary entry | 40–60 words |
| 8 | Supermarket shopping list, table reservation and food orders | practical notes |
| 9 | Directions guide, public transport message and arrival note | 50–70 words |
| 10 | Weather report, outdoor excursion plan and modal sentences | 50–70 words |
| 11 | Sick note / absence email, doctor appointment and symptoms | 40–60 words |
| 12 | Full Goethe A1 Schreiben (Formular + official exam email) | timed exam format |

Write a first draft, check against `Writing/SELF_CORRECTION_CHECKLIST.md`, then produce a clean second version.
