# Week 10 Writing Practice

Write 50–80 words appropriate to the topic **Weather, Seasons & Modal Verbs**.

Required elements:
- Address all required content points.
- Use at least four vocabulary items from Week 10.
- Include one time or place expression.
- Include one polite request or question.
- Use at least one connector (*und, aber, denn, deshalb*).
- Check finite verb positions and capitalise all nouns.

Create a clean, corrected second draft after using `Writing/SELF_CORRECTION_CHECKLIST.md`.
