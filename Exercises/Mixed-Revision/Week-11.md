# Week 11 Mixed Revision

## 30-Minute Checkpoint

1. **Recall:** 15 random vocabulary items from Week 11 (with articles) — 5 min.
2. **Grammar:** Build five sentences using target grammar structures — 5 min.
3. **Listening:** Listen to one daily track and extract key details — 5 min.
4. **Reading:** Read one passage in `Weeks/Week-11/Reading.md` and answer questions — 5 min.
5. **Speaking:** Speak for one minute on the weekly milestone — 5 min.
6. **Writing:** Write a 4-sentence message on the topic — 5 min.

Rate each area: *Independent* / *With Help* / *Needs Review*. Focus on your weakest skill before moving forward.
