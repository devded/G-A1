# Week 12 Speaking Practice

**Milestone:** Master exam strategies for Listening (Hören), Reading (Lesen), Writing (Schreiben), and Speaking (Sprechen), and complete two full A1 Mock Exams.

## Prompt Ladder

1. Answer five targeted questions related to **Goethe A1 Exam Prep & Mock Exams**.
2. Connect your answers into a continuous 30-second spoken response.
3. Speak for 60–90 seconds using only keywords (no script).
4. Perform one interactive question-and-answer exchange.
5. Simulate changing one key detail (e.g. time, price, destination).
6. Use one clarification phrase (*Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?*).

Useful patterns: `Entschuldigung, wo ist …?`, `Ich möchte bitte …`, `Wie viel kostet …?`, `Wann fährt … ab?`, `Ich kann/muss heute …`
