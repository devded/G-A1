# Week 11 Vocabulary Practice

## Recall Drills

Write the German word, including article and plural where applicable:

1. the head — মাথা
2. the arm — হাত (বাহু)
3. the leg — পা
4. the stomach / belly — পেট
5. the back — পিঠ
6. the heart — হৃদয় / হার্ট
7. the throat / neck — গলা / ঘাড়
8. the tooth — দাঁত
9. the hand — হাত (কবজি থেকে আঙুল)
10. the foot — পায়ের পাতা
11. the eye — চোখ
12. the ear — কান

## Contextual Use

Choose six words from the list above and write one connected mini-text (4–6 sentences). Underline the target vocabulary items. Then read your text aloud twice with correct pronunciation and intonation.
