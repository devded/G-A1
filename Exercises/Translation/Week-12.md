# Week 12 Controlled Translation

Translate into German using only familiar A1 structures:

1. Where is the train station, and how do I get there?
2. Take the bus number 4 and get off at the town hall.
3. Today it is sunny and warm, but tomorrow it will rain.
4. I want to visit my friend at the weekend.
5. I have a headache and need an appointment with the doctor.
6. Can you please write down the address for me?
7. We met yesterday at six o'clock in the restaurant.
8. The train has a fifteen-minute delay.

Then translate three of your German sentences into natural Bengali and compare the grammatical structures.
