# Week 11 Listening Practice

Audio: [`Week-11-Day-74.wav`](../../Assets/Audio/Week-11-Day-74.wav)

1. Listen once to identify the overall situation and participants.
2. Note all numbers, times, dates, prices, or locations mentioned.
3. Write down five key content words you heard clearly.
4. State the main message in one English or Bengali sentence.
5. Transcribe one full sentence from the audio.
6. Shadow the entire track aloud twice.

Transcript and solutions are in `Weeks/Week-11/Listening.md`.
