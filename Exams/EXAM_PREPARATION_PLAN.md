# 12-Week Goethe A1 Exam Preparation Plan

## Week 1: Sounds, Alphabet and Personal Introductions
- Main action: Master the German alphabet, pronunciation, spelling, and self-introductions.
- Milestone: Introduce yourself, spell personal information and understand another beginner’s introduction.
- Save one speaking recording and one personal profile writing sample.

## Week 2: Personal Information, Identity and Languages
- Main action: Fill out personal data, talk about origin, languages, and contact details.
- Milestone: Discuss origin, languages, contact details and personal background.
- Save one speaking recording and one registration form sample.

## Week 3: Family Members and Possessive Determiners
- Main action: Describe family members, relationships, and use possessives (*mein, dein, sein, ihr*).
- Milestone: Describe family, work or study, possessions and relationships.
- Save one speaking recording and one family description paragraph.

## Week 4: House, Rooms, Furniture and Plurals
- Main action: Identify rooms, furniture, plural nouns, and use *haben* / *sein*.
- Milestone: Describe living space, apartment rooms, furniture and plurals.
- Save one speaking recording and one apartment description message.

## Week 5: Work, Occupations and Numbers 0-100
- Main action: State professions, workplace activities, and count fluently up to 100.
- Milestone: State jobs, workplaces, age and count up to 100.
- Save one speaking recording and one job description email.

## Week 6: Time, Daily Routine and Separable Verbs
- Main action: Tell clock time (formal/informal), discuss schedules, and use separable verbs.
- Milestone: Tell time, describe a daily routine and arrange a simple meeting.
- Save one speaking recording and one schedule message.

## Week 7: Hobbies, Free Time and Sentence Structure
- Main action: Discuss hobbies, sports, express preferences with *gern*, and master Verb-Second rule.
- Milestone: Talk about free time, sports activities and sentence structure (V2).
- Save one speaking recording and one diary entry sample.

## Week 8: Food, Grocery Shopping and Accusative Case
- Main action: Buy groceries, ask prices, order food, and master masculine accusative (*einen/keinen*).
- Milestone: Express food preferences, grocery shop and master accusative case.
- Save one speaking recording and one shopping dialogue sample.

## Week 9: City, Directions, Transportation and Dative
- Main action: Ask for/give directions, read transit maps, and use Dative contractions (*im, am, beim, zum, zur*).
- Milestone: Ask for and give directions, understand public transport timetables, and navigate city travel independently.
- Save one speaking recording and one directions message.

## Week 10: Weather, Nature, Seasons and Modal Verbs
- Main action: Describe weather with impersonal *es*, and use all 6 modal verbs with sentence brackets.
- Milestone: Describe weather conditions, talk about seasons and free-time plans, and use modal verbs with correct sentence brackets.
- Save one speaking recording and one weekend excursion plan email.

## Week 11: Health, Body Parts and Present Perfect Tense
- Main action: Describe symptoms, doctor/pharmacy dialogues, and express past events with *Perfekt*.
- Milestone: Describe health symptoms, make doctor appointments, understand pharmacy instructions, and express past events using the Perfekt tense.
- Save one speaking recording and one absence/sick-note email.

## Week 12: Goethe A1 Exam Strategy, Timed Mocks & Mastery
- Main action: Complete official exam strategies for Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen, and complete Mock Exam 1 & 2.
- Milestone: Complete realistic Goethe A1 exam tasks across Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, and Sprechen within official time limits.
- Save Mock Exam 1 and Mock Exam 2 complete scorecards and error logs.
