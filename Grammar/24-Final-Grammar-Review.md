# 24 — Final German A1 Grammar Review & Master Checklist

## 1. Master Grammar Checklist for Goethe A1 Exam

Before taking the A1 examination or completing your studies, verify that you can confidently use each of the following structures:

- [ ] **Nouns & Articles:** Identify *der / die / das* and form standard plurals with *die*.
- [ ] **Pronouns:** Conjugate regular and irregular verbs for *ich, du, er/sie/es, wir, ihr, sie, Sie*.
- [ ] **Verbs *sein* & *haben*:** Form statements of identity, age, possession, and condition.
- [ ] **Verb-Second Rule (V2):** Maintain the conjugated verb at Position 2, including when starting with time or place (*Inversion*).
- [ ] **Questions:** Form W-Questions (*Wo, Wann, Wie, Was, Wer, Warum*) and Yes/No questions.
- [ ] **Negation:** Correctly choose between *kein* (for nouns) and *nicht* (for verbs, adjectives, specific phrases).
- [ ] **Accusative Case:** Accurately decline masculine nouns (*den, einen, keinen, meinen*) after verbs like *haben, kaufen, brauchen, essen, trinken*.
- [ ] **Dative Case:** Use basic Dative articles (*dem, der, dem, den + n*) and prepositions (*aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu*).
- [ ] **Modal Verbs:** Conjugate all 6 modal verbs (*können, müssen, dürfen, wollen, sollen, möchten*) and place the infinitive at sentence end.
- [ ] **Separable Verbs:** Separate prefixes (*aufstehen → stehe ... auf*) in present tense main clauses.
- [ ] **Past Tense (Perfekt):** Form past sentences using *haben* / *sein* + *Partizip II* (*Ich habe gelernt / Ich bin gefahren*).
- [ ] **Time & Dates:** Express clock time (official and informal *halb/viertel*) and dates with *am ersten...*.
- [ ] **Imperative & Requests:** Ask politely for help or objects (*Können Sie mir bitte...*).
