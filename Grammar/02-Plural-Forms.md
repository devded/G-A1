# 02 — Plural Forms of Nouns (*Pluralbildung*)

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

In German, **ALL plural nouns take the definite article `die`** in the Nominative case, regardless of whether the singular was *der, die,* or *das*.

Unlike English (which simply adds *-s* or *-es*), German has **5 primary plural patterns**, often accompanied by an **Umlaut (ä, ö, ü)** on the root vowel.

---

## 2. The 5 German Plural Patterns

| Pattern | Ending | Singular → Plural | Typical Gender / Noun Group |
|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|
| **Group 1** | **-e / ¨-e** | *der Tag → die Tag**e***<br>*der Stuhl → die St**ü**hl**e***<br>*das Brot → die Brot**e*** | Very common for masculine and neuter 1-syllable nouns. |
| **Group 2** | **-(e)n** | *die Frau → die Frau**en***<br>*die Lampe → die Lampe**n***<br>*der Student → die Student**en*** | **Over 90% of feminine nouns** take *-(e)n*. Never takes an Umlaut! |
| **Group 3** | **-er / ¨-er** | *das Kind → die Kind**er***<br>*das Buch → die B**ü**ch**er***<br>*der Mann → die M**ä**nn**er*** | Almost all neuter nouns with 1 syllable; frequently adds an Umlaut. |
| **Group 4** | **— / ¨— (no ending)** | *der Lehrer → die Lehrer*<br>*das Fenster → die Fenster*<br>*der Apfel → die **Ä**pfel*<br>*der Vater → die V**ä**ter* | Nouns ending in **-er, -el, -en, -chen, -lein**. Masculine often adds Umlaut. |
| **Group 5** | **-s** | *das Auto → die Auto**s***<br>*das Hotel → die Hotel**s***<br>*das Sofa → die Sofa**s*** | Foreign loanwords (English/French) and words ending in full vowels (*a, i, o, u*). |

---

## 3. Detailed Breakdown by Gender

### Feminine Plurals (`die` → `die`)
- If the noun ends in `-e`, `-in`, `-ung`, `-heit`, `-keit`, `-schaft`, `-ion`, `-tät`, it almost **always takes `-n` or `-en`**:
  - *die Blume → die Blumen*
  - *die Lehrerin → die Lehrerin**nen*** (Note the double *-nen* for female professions!)
  - *die Zeitung → die Zeitungen*
  - *die Nation → die Nationen*

### Masculine Plurals (`der` → `die`)
- Most 1-syllable masculine nouns take **`-e` + Umlaut**:
  - *der Ball → die Bälle*
  - *der Baum → die Bäume*
  - *der Zug → die Züge*
- Words ending in `-er`, `-el`, `-en` take **no ending**, but root vowels *a, o, u* often get Umlauts:
  - *der Bruder → die Brüder*
  - *der Garten → die Gärten*
  - *der Vogel → die Vögel*

### Neuter Plurals (`das` → `die`)
- Most 1-syllable neuter nouns take **`-er` + Umlaut**:
  - *das Haus → die Häuser*
  - *das Bild → die Bilder*
  - *das Glas → die Gläser*
  - *das Land → die Länder*

---

## 4. Special Nouns: Singular-Only vs Plural-Only

### Nouns used ONLY in the Singular (*Singulariatantum*):
- *das Geld* (money)
- *die Milch* (milk)
- *der Durst* (thirst), *der Hunger* (hunger)
- *das Fleisch* (meat), *das Obst* (fruit), *das Gemüse* (vegetables)

### Nouns used ONLY in the Plural (*Pluraliatantum*):
- *die Eltern* (parents)
- *die Geschwister* (siblings)
- *die Leute* (people)
- *die Ferien* (school vacation), *die Kosten* (costs)

### Exercise 1: Form the plural for each noun (including the article `die`)
1. der Tisch → ___
2. die Tasche → ___
3. das Kind → ___
4. der Apfel → ___
5. das Auto → ___
6. die Ärztin → ___
7. das Buch → ___
8. der Computer → ___

<details><summary>Click here for Answer Key & Explanations</summary>

1. **die Tische** (Group 1: adds *-e*)
2. **die Taschen** (Group 2: feminine ending in *-e* adds *-n*)
3. **die Kinder** (Group 3: neuter adds *-er*)
4. **die Äpfel** (Group 4: ending in *-el* takes no suffix, vowel changes *A → Ä*)
5. **die Autos** (Group 5: foreign word ending in vowel adds *-s*)
6. **die Ärztinnen** (Group 2: female profession *-in* doubles to *-innen*)
7. **die Bücher** (Group 3: neuter adds *-er* + Umlaut *u → ü*)
8. **die Computer** (Group 4: masculine ending in *-er* takes no change)
